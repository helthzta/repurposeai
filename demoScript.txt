FASTGROCERY – EXAM NIGHT SAVER

CONTINUITY LOCK (APPLIES TO ENTIRE VIDEO)

Male, 21 years old.
Medium brown Indian skin tone.
Slim build.
Messy black hair.
Rectangular black-rim glasses.
Navy blue hoodie with visible wrinkles.

FRIEND 1:
Male, 22 years old.
Medium brown Indian skin tone.
Average build.
Messy hair.
Oversized grey graphic t-shirt.
Energetic body language.

FRIEND 2:
Male, 23 years old.
Medium brown Indian skin tone.
Clean haircut.
Black polo shirt.
Calm posture.

LOCATION:
Small Indian dorm room.
Grey walls.
Engineering textbooks.
Notebooks.
Loose papers.
Pens.
Single study lamp.
Messy study desk.

FIRST HALF COLOR:
Cold.
Grey.
Desaturated.

SECOND HALF COLOR:
Warm.
Vibrant.
Yellow highlights.

SCENE 1 (0-4s)

CAMERA:
Handheld extreme close-up on Student.

ACTION:

Student studies intensely.

He flips pages rapidly.

Eyes appear tired.

His stomach contracts slightly.

Hands grip study notes.

Only Student is visible.

No other character appears in frame.

AUDIO:

Loud stomach rumble.

DIALOGUE:

"Bhai, bhook lagi hai!"

LIP-SYNC RULE:

Only Student delivers this line.
No other voice overlaps.

---

SCENE 2 (4-7s)

CAMERA:
Quick pull-back to medium shot.

ACTION:

Friend 1 enters rapidly from frame left.

He throws three separate objects one after another:

FIRST:
Small flour bag.

SECOND:
Metal salt canister.

THIRD:
Raw potato.

PHYSICS RULES:

Flour bag strikes desk first.

Bag partially tears open on impact.

Flour bursts upward.

Fine flour particles spread naturally.

Salt canister lands second.

Metal canister bounces once.

Rolls across desk.

Gradually slows from friction.

Potato lands last.

Potato slides across papers.

Stops against Student's notes.

Objects must obey realistic gravity and momentum.

Student remains seated.

Student flinches naturally.

Glasses remain secure.

AUDIO:

Whoosh.
Desk impact.
Metal clang.
Paper rustling.

DIALOGUE:

Friend 1 only:

"Aata le! Namak le! Bana le kuch!"

LIP-SYNC RULE:

Only Friend 1 speaks.
Student remains silent.

CAMERA:
Medium shot.

ACTION:

Student now has light flour dust on:

Hair.
Shoulders.
Sleeves.
Desk surface.

Student picks up the raw potato using his right hand.

Friend 1 stands beside desk.

Student looks exhausted.

DIALOGUE:

Student only:

"Abe! Exam hai, 40 min ka time nahi hai!"

LIP-SYNC RULE:

Only Student speaks.
Friend 1 remains silent.

Bright yellow vest.
Black t-shirt.

Visible relief.
